Sample Security+ MCQ

Question: A company wants to ensure a server certificate was issued by a trusted authority and has not expired. Which concept is most relevant?

A. Tokenization
B. Certificate validation
C. Data masking
D. Geofencing

Answer: B. Certificate validation

Certificate validation checks trust chain, expiration, revocation status, and whether the certificate matches the requested host.
