Sample Security+ question

Question: Which control best reduces the risk of a stolen password being enough to access an account?

A. MFA
B. Open guest WiFi
C. Plaintext storage
D. Shared administrator accounts

Answer: A. MFA

Multi-factor authentication requires another factor beyond the password, reducing the impact of password theft.
