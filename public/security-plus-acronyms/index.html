AAA

Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting. Exam clue: identity, permission, and activity tracking.

ACL

Access Control List. Exam clue: rule list that allows or denies traffic, users, or objects.

MFA

Multi-Factor Authentication. Exam clue: two or more proof types, such as password plus authenticator app.
