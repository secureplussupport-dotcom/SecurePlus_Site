CIA triad

Confidentiality protects secrecy, integrity protects correctness, and availability keeps systems usable.

Least privilege

Users and services should receive only the access they need to complete the task.

Defense in depth

Use layered controls so one failed control does not expose the whole environment.

How to study notes
