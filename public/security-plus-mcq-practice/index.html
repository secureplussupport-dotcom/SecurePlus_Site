Sample Security+ MCQ

Question: Which security control category is primarily intended to discourage a threat actor from attempting an attack?

A. Detective
B. Corrective
C. Deterrent
D. Recovery

Answer: C. Deterrent

Deterrent controls discourage attacks. Detective controls identify activity, corrective controls fix issues, and recovery controls restore service.
