## Whole-card semantic decisions

**AP / WAP — wireless_access_point_vocabulary.** Qualify the explicitly expanded access-point vocabulary for an infrastructure WLAN connecting clients through a distribution system, commonly wired. The slash groups names for this device role rather than two independently assessed controls.

- The supplied WAP expansion fixes the intended device meaning in this card; the acronym alone in another context is not sufficient to identify this role.
- An AP is not inherently a router, firewall or secure configuration. Wired connection describes the common infrastructure case, not every possible mesh/backhaul topology; association does not prove authorization or segmentation.

**ARP — local_protocol_to_link_address_vocabulary.** Qualify the common IPv4/Ethernet local-link mapping from a protocol address to a link-layer address; this is vocabulary, not a packet capture, trustworthy binding or universal all-IP resolution mechanism.

- ARP does not allocate an IP address like DHCP or resolve a domain name like DNS. Routing chooses the local next hop to resolve; a remote destination does not imply an end-to-end remote MAC lookup.
- The sentence is bounded to the conventional IPv4/Ethernet case; IPv6 neighbor discovery and non-Ethernet variants are outside this card. No authenticated mapping, poisoning detection or successful exchange is established.

Candidate **sy0701.term.arp** remains unapproved instructional support of weight0.

**DHCP — host_configuration_allocation_vocabulary.** Qualify automatic host address/configuration delivery as the usual DHCP role. Allocation can be automatic, dynamic or administrator-assigned; the card does not promise every address is a fresh dynamic lease.

- DHCP supplies address/configuration parameters; ARP performs local link resolution and DNS name-data lookup. Static/manual allocation and lease renewal are not distinguished by a short name recall.
- The inspected DHCPv4 background does not approve DHCPv6, guaranteed service availability or a trusted server. No lease, relay, rogue-server control or working configuration is tested.

Candidate **sy0701.term.dhcp** remains unapproved instructional support of weight0.

**DMZ — screened_subnet_vocabulary.** Qualify the screened subnet/public-service separation pattern as an architecture description, conditional on actual routing and filtering policy rather than guaranteed safety from the label.

- Public service placement can reduce direct exposure of internal systems, but permitted flows, hardened hosts and enforced boundaries determine protection. Separate subnet labels alone do not establish effective isolation.
- A DMZ is not necessarily an air gap or a single mandated topology. The card supplies no designed policy, permitted-flow analysis or secure deployed architecture.

Candidate **sy0701.term.dmz** remains unapproved instructional support of weight0.

**DNS — domain_address_lookup_vocabulary.** Qualify domain-name-to-address lookup as one common DNS function, without narrowing the Domain Name System to only IP translations or promising a unique/live answer.

- DNS associates typed data with names; multiple records, aliases, cache state or no answer may occur. It is different from DHCP address allocation and ARP local-link address resolution.
- The short definition does not teach authoritative/recursive roles, validate returned data, prove DNSSEC, or imply encrypted queries. No lookup, record change or available service is executed.

Candidate **sy0701.term.dns** remains unapproved instructional support of weight0.

**FACL — filesystem_permission_list_vocabulary.** Qualify the explicitly expanded filesystem access-control list as permissions for files/directories. This is an object-access vocabulary item in a broad deck, not a network forwarding ACL.

- Filesystem ACL semantics depend on the platform, identity, permissions and inheritance model. The inspected Linux access/default ACL behavior is illustrative; it does not establish the semantics of every operating system.
- ACL entries are not encryption, network packet rules or proof of effective least privilege. No access evaluation, inheritance change or privileged-user restriction is performed.

**FTPS / SFTP — tls_ftp_vs_ssh_file_transfer_vocabulary.** Qualify the explicit transport distinction: FTPS adds TLS to FTP, while SFTP uses SSH transport. These are different file-transfer protocols, not interchangeable labels for FTP over SSH.

- TLS/SSH protection depends on negotiated configuration and endpoint verification. FTPS control and data protection are separate policy choices; its historical TLS reference does not recommend obsolete TLS versions.
- SFTP does not become FTPS by using an FTP-like interface, and neither implies protected storage or uncompromised endpoints. No connection, transfer, certificate or host key is verified; the grouped card adds no independent family.

Candidate **sy0701.term.ftps-sftp** remains unapproved instructional support of weight0.

**ICMP — ip_control_error_echo_vocabulary.** Qualify IP control/error messages with echo-based ping as a familiar example. The definition names a broader protocol role rather than treating every ICMP message as ping.

- ICMP is not a TCP/UDP application service. Error reporting and echo replies do not guarantee delivery; filtering or absent replies do not establish that a host is down.
- The inspected ICMPv4 introduction bounds this review; ICMPv6 neighbor discovery and router behavior are outside the short card. No reachability, attack or filtering policy is observed.

**IPsec — ip_security_suite_capability_vocabulary.** Qualify authentication/integrity and encryption as capabilities of the IPsec suite, commonly used in VPNs. The sentence is a suite-level summary, not a guarantee that every IPsec packet or association uses encryption.

- AH offers integrity/origin authentication without confidentiality; ESP can also operate without encryption. Effective services depend on protocol, association mode, endpoints and selected options.
- Transport/tunnel deployment, traffic selectors, keys and policy require separate decisions. No active VPN, protected flow, algorithm recommendation or complete architecture competency follows from vocabulary recall.

**LDAP — directory_query_update_vocabulary.** Qualify the directory-access protocol role for requesting searches and modifications. The service applies authorization/schema restrictions; query/update capability does not imply an unrestricted successful operation.

- LDAP is a protocol rather than the directory database or one specific vendor product. Directory attributes, authentication and authorization are separate concerns; the short definition proves none of them.

- A label or provider private-network service is not cryptographic confidentiality or automatically secure isolation. No route, label distribution, forwarding table, VPN or performance result is tested.

- Basic address translation and address-plus-port translation differ; PAT rewrites/maps ports rather than merely observing them. Mapping state and permitted return traffic are distinct from routing and address allocation.
- NAT can involve other address realms and is not inherently encryption or a firewall policy. The card proves neither all ports are safe nor all inbound traffic is blocked; no translation table or real flow is evaluated.
