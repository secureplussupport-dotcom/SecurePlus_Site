### Perimeter Security

Historical boundary-focused protections are recognizable. HOLD retained: treating greater internal trust as part of the whole category overgeneralizes; perimeter controls can coexist with Zero Trust. No source rewrite or inherited hold resolution.

### Hybrid Cloud

Connected private/on-premises and public-service wording is a loose deployment shorthand. HOLD retained: ordinary on-premises systems plus public services do not establish distinct cloud infrastructures bound for portability; a public component is not universal in the formal hybrid taxonomy.

### Public Cloud

Ownership and internet access are observable characteristics, not the key deployment distinction. HOLD retained: missing open provision to the general public; third-party hosted private/community clouds can also use internet access. Public cloud does not make every customer resource/data public.

### Anonymization

Removing identifying associations has a privacy goal. HOLD new: irreversible removal of explicit links does not establish an absolute cannot-be-reidentified outcome with auxiliary data and quasi-identifiers. Distinguish the intended anonymization goal from a demonstrated privacy result.

### Data at Rest

A stored copy is the data-state being named. Qualified introductory stored-data vocabulary, including files, databases and backups. State does not guarantee encryption; a stored copy can coexist with transmitted/processed copies and authorized-session risks.

### Data in Transit / Motion

Communication paths carry a moving copy of data. Qualified transport-state synonyms. Do not infer encryption, authentication or endpoint/storage protection from motion, cable or wireless alone.

### Data in Use

Processing in memory, registers or an active application identifies the use state. Qualified processing-state vocabulary. Disk/transport encryption is not an assurance for decrypted processing; no confidential-computing implementation or proof is supplied.

### Data Masking / Obfuscation

Concealing or altering visible sensitive characters reduces what the user can see. Qualified narrow data-display transformation. Masking is not universal anonymization, encryption or complete reidentification prevention; obfuscation is broader than this data-character use case. No canonical equivalence of every obfuscation technique.

### Pseudo-anonymization

The described pseudonym-plus-separate-map scheme can restore an identity association. Qualified reversible pseudonymization example under the source alias. Preserve separate-map/authorization context; protect mapping and linkage risks, and do not classify pseudonymous records as irreversibly anonymous or claim only authorized attackers can reidentify. No formal/legal label approval.

### Tokenization

Sensitive values can be replaced with surrogates. HOLD new: no useful value outside the vault conflates inability to recover the original value with legitimate application/payment utility. A vault example does not establish universal harmlessness, authorization or attacker uselessness.

### Air Gap

Physical disconnection describes an isolation interface/network design. Qualified physical-isolation vocabulary. Manual media transfer and human-controlled bridging can remain risks; no malware-free, leakage-free or merely logical-firewall isolation claim.

### East-West Traffic

Lateral communication within the supplied internal/data-center scope is the traffic pattern. Qualified relative architectural direction, not geography or a security verdict. A defined topology/boundary is required; internal traffic still needs authorization and inspection where appropriate.

### Extranet

Limited external-partner/customer access to a controlled private environment is the use case. Qualified audience/access vocabulary. Trusted-party wording does not grant unrestricted internal trust; policies, identity and least privilege remain necessary. Can overlap other network zones.

### Intranet

An organization uses its private internal network for internal work. Qualified internal-network vocabulary. Employee use is a common audience, not an exclusive eligibility rule or proof every user/device/application is trustworthy.

### Jump Box / Bastion Host

A hardened administrative entry point reaches a protected or separate zone. Qualified administrative bastion/jump use case only. Bastion can name broader hardened/exposed roles; no global synonym equivalence, internet/public-IP requirement or guaranteed safe administration. It is not a whole security zone or the target asset.

### North-South Traffic

Communication entering/leaving the stated network boundary is the pattern. Qualified relative ingress/egress vocabulary, not compass direction or mandatory malicious/external status. Keep its chosen boundary distinct from lateral communication within that frame.

### Screened Subnet

A controlled intermediate subnet illustrates a common DMZ layout. Qualified introductory separation pattern, not literal geography or automatic isolation. Policy enforcement and controlled paths matter; naming a subnet does not prove access restrictions or require one universal firewall layout.
