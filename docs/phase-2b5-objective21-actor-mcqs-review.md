## Official scope and primary-source checks

[CompTIA SY0-701 v7 §2.1](https://lecbyo.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/cf25ec24b8a511ef9ecbb69c0f9687be) lists six actor types and separately lists access relationship, resources/capability and motivations. **APT is not an explicit actor bullet**. Its supplied profile can give inferred actor-attribute context; it is not automatically a precise official actor category. Technique, motive, capability and sponsorship are different dimensions, and all may overlap. The selected MCQs assess bounded recognition rather than incident investigation or the entire objective.

[NIST's APT definitions](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/advanced_persistent_threat) include significant resources/expertise and repeated adaptive persistence. They do not prescribe an11/14/18-month threshold or require state sponsorship/zero-days. The editorial best-fit options are not proof of those missing dimensions. **m122/m252 precise actor linking stays held** because targeted theft and long dwell alone do not establish capability/resources. m013 explicitly supplies these additional premises, though its feedback still needs the whole profile rather than a duration shortcut.

[NIST's insider definitions](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/insider_threat) describe authorised access used wittingly or unwittingly to cause harm. The effective m439 expressly confirms employee intent; an export schedule alone would not do that. [MITRE's software model](https://attack.mitre.org/software/) includes public/commercial tools used by defenders, testers and adversaries, so a public tool name alone cannot prove low skill. [MITRE's group model](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/index.html) preserves overlapping activity-cluster associations rather than exact alias/identity equivalence. Its [APT38 profile](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0082/) illustrates state sponsorship alongside financial operations; this source check rejects exclusive money-versus-state inference, not an attribution of any fictional item to that group. No real campaign, actor, account or telemetry was investigated.
