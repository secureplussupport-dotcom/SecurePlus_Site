### GDPR

Authored expansion: General Data Protection Regulation. Meaning: EU privacy law that protects personal data of people in the EU and EEA. No authored example.

Recognize the GDPR name and its EU/EEA personal-data protection context. Qualified introductory purpose vocabulary, not an exhaustive territorial-scope rule. The sentence does not say only people at one location are covered; establishment/processing context and other conditions cannot be inferred from nationality, storage location or this short card. No actual applicability, duty, lawful basis, transfer or compliance conclusion.

### HIPAA

Authored expansion: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. Meaning: U.S. law that sets privacy and security requirements for protected health information. No authored example.

Recognize the U.S. legislative framework associated with health-information privacy and security. Qualified umbrella-law purpose, not a complete HIPAA definition or a universal rule for every health record. Covered contexts matter; the Privacy Rule and electronic-PHI Security Rule have different scope. No current obligations, record classification, permitted disclosure, safeguards or compliance assessment.

### PCI DSS

Authored expansion: Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard. Meaning: Security standard for organizations that store, process, or transmit payment-card data. No authored example.

Recognize the payment-account-data security standard and its common data-handling audience. Qualified purpose/audience vocabulary, not an exhaustive applicability test. Entities affecting cardholder-data-environment security can also be in scope without directly holding card data. A named industry standard is not a law, version-specific assessment, certification or evidence that controls work.

### PHI

Authored expansion: Protected Health Information. Meaning: Health information that can identify a person. No authored example.

Identifiable health information is broader than a precisely scoped protected-health-information category. HOLD: the isolated meaning supplies identifiability but not the HIPAA covered-entity/business-associate context or relevant exclusions. A learner could classify all identifiable health data as PHI. This judgment comes from the card itself; the separate held glossary does not automatically hold it and neighboring HIPAA wording cannot repair it. No real record or legal applicability is classified.

### PII

Authored expansion: Personally Identifiable Information. Meaning: Information that can identify, contact, or locate a person. No authored example.

Recognize information capable of identifying or linking contact/location details to an individual. Qualified introductory identity-information vocabulary. Identifiability/linkability depends on available context and combination, rather than a fixed universal list or an assumption that all contact strings identify one person. This is not a universal legal definition, proof of anonymity or a classification of real data; PII, GDPR personal data and HIPAA PHI are not automatically coextensive.

### Gamification

Authored expansion: Gamification. Meaning: Use of points, challenges, or competition to make security training more engaging. No authored example.

Recognize using game elements such as points/challenges/competition in training design. Qualified mechanism and intended engagement purpose, not a measured improvement, healthy competition guarantee, learning mastery or demonstrated secure behavior. Participation/reward totals and game performance require evaluation and do not independently prove reduced risk.

### Phishing Simulations

Authored expansion: Phishing Simulations. Meaning: Controlled phishing tests used to measure reporting behavior and provide quick retraining. No authored example.

Recognize controlled phishing exercises used to observe reporting and provide follow-up learning. Qualified training use case; reporting/click rates depend on message difficulty, audience, observation windows and controls. Retraining is an intended programme action, not guaranteed effective or a complete measure of real-world behavior. No messages, employee tests, credentials or telemetry are collected.

### Role-Based Security Training

Authored expansion: Role-Based Security Training. Meaning: Security training tailored to a person's job duties, such as developers, finance staff, or executives. No authored example.

Recognize training tailored to the knowledge and security tasks needed for different jobs. Qualified job-specific learning purpose. This is distinct from role-based access control or automatic privileges; a job title alone does not define all skills, authority or successful completion. No assessed learner competence, role mapping or security outcome.

### Right to Audit Clause

Authored expansion: Right to Audit Clause. Meaning: Contract term that gives an organization permission to inspect or audit a vendor's controls and processes. No authored example.

Recognize a contract provision granting a defined inspection/audit permission. Qualified contract-purpose vocabulary. Scope, notice, evidence access, confidentiality, third-party rights, lawful authority and practical enforcement depend on actual terms/context. Naming the clause neither grants unlimited access nor proves a right was negotiated, exercised or satisfied. No contract drafting, enforceability or legal conclusion.

### Supply Chain Risk

Authored expansion: Supply Chain Risk. Meaning: Risk introduced through external software, hardware, vendors, service providers, or business partners. No authored example.

Recognize risk arising through external products, providers and business relationships in this third-party card context. Qualified external-supply-chain focus, not a claim that all supply-chain risk originates externally or is malicious. Product lifecycle, dependencies and internal management also matter. Vendor diversity/labels do not guarantee integrity, availability or transfer organizational accountability.

## Bounded semantic and legal review

Eight reviewer counterexamples bind the applicable source revisions; none is an operational, legal, employee-training or real-data test.

- regional_label_not_applicability_test: A location/citizenship or storage-location fact alone does not determine the regulation application to a processing context.
- identifiability_not_covered_context: Identifiable health information without covered-context facts is insufficient to classify a real record as HIPAA PHI.
- identity_linkability_context: Available linked information can change whether a datum distinguishes or traces a person; a short list is not a universal legal classification.
- handling_not_exhaustive_cde_scope: A provider can affect CDE security without directly storing cardholder data; a label supplies no compliance evidence.
- points_or_low_click_rate_not_effectiveness: High point totals or an easy simulation with low click rate do not establish lower real-world risk or mastery.
- training_role_not_access_grant: Job-tailored instruction does not itself grant system privileges or demonstrate competence.
- clause_label_not_unlimited_permission: A provision with defined scope/notice is not a blanket right to inspect every supplier system or a completed audit.
- assessment_not_elimination_or_transfer: A supplier assessment may identify remaining exposure; it does not eliminate dependency risk or transfer accountability.

GDPR/HIPAA/PCI DSS are qualified for basic name/purpose recognition, not exhaustive scope. GDPR geographic shorthand is not interpreted as an only-these-people rule; HIPAA umbrella purpose does not make Privacy/Security Rule coverage identical; PCI data handling is a common audience rather than every applicable CDE-impact relationship.
