### CISO

Recognize the senior role leading organizational information-security and associated risk work. Qualified information-security leadership vocabulary. ### CSIRT

Current displayed expansion: Computer Security Incident Response Team. Current definition: Team responsible for triage, containment, mitigation, and closure of security incidents.

Recognize a team coordinating or delivering security-incident response work. Qualified incident-management purpose: triage, containment, mitigation and case closure depend on mandate and resources. ### DER

Recognize binary ASN.1 encoding rules commonly applied to X.509 certificate structures. ### FRR

Recognize false rejection of genuine biometric verification transactions. Qualified error-direction vocabulary. ### MTTD
