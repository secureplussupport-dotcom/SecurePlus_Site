- concurrent_action_not_stated_check_use: Two withdrawal actions do not establish which resource was checked, changed and later used.
- authenticated_not_authorized: A verified identity can still lack permission for the requested resource.
- approved_choice_not_ownership: Employee purchase from an approved list supplies choice without corporate ownership.

- official_off_store_distribution: A developer or enterprise can distribute its own application outside an app store.
- demand_not_restoration: An extortion demand can coexist with continuing encrypted or blocked data.
- null_profile_no_confidentiality: A stipulated NULL-encryption profile does not encrypt its RTP payload.
- historical_none_not_secure_recommendation: The historical optional none cipher supplies no confidentiality; this is not a proposed configuration.
- mpls_private_not_crypto: A stipulated BGP/MPLS VPN can have private reachability without cryptographic privacy.
- forgery_not_guaranteed_disconnect_or_reassociation: Negotiated robust-frame protection and client selection/authentication prevent assuming the illustrated outcome.
- plaintext_listener_not_relay: A passive receiver of exposed radio traffic need not mediate the connection.
