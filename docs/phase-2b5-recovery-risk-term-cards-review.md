### Cold Site

Basic facility readiness differs from installed operational IT readiness. Qualified site-class vocabulary: utilities and physical space require equipment, configuration, data, connectivity and staff preparation. No inferred restoration deadline, ready data or achieved RTO/RPO.

### Fault Tolerance

A system can continue operating through covered component failures. Qualified capability within its designed fault model. One or more does not mean every combination, all components, unlimited failures or zero degraded performance; detection, independence and continuity requirements matter. Not equivalent to load balancing, backup or geographic separation.

### Geographic Dispersal

Separating systems geographically can reduce shared local exposure. HOLD: across-region placement alone does not ensure one local disaster cannot take down the whole service. Shared dependencies, data/traffic failover, capacity and independent operability remain unspecified; the outcome is overstated rather than expressed as a risk-reduction goal.

### Hot Site

The stipulated recovery facility has comparatively high operational readiness. Qualified readiness label, not proof of a particular RTO/RPO, synchronized clean data or instant failover. Hardware/software/data ready is the introductory scenario premise; dependencies, activation, testing and data age determine real recovery.

### Load Balancing

Traffic distribution can improve capacity utilization and availability. Qualified distribution mechanism and intended benefit, not a measured improvement or fault tolerance by itself. Health checks, suitable spare capacity, controller reliability and dependencies matter. No actual balancing algorithm, topology or universal latency benefit.

### RAID 0

Non-redundant striping is the level distinction. Qualified performance-oriented striping versus redundancy. Performance only is the teaching contrast, not a guarantee of faster work or denial of capacity aggregation. Drive failure can lose data; no backup, recovery point or durable redundancy.

### RAID 1

Mirrored current copies supply disk redundancy. Qualified mirroring under healthy surviving copies and working control paths. Logical deletion/corruption can propagate to mirrors; replicas are not independent historical backups. No all-fault, controller or site survivability guarantee.

### RAID 5

Striping with distributed parity supports a single missing disk under the ordinary intact-array model. Qualified introductory minimum-three-drive/single-disk-failure distinction. Further failures, unreadable sectors, controller faults and rebuild conditions can defeat recovery; not a tested array, backup or RPO guarantee.

### RAID 6

Dual-parity striping supports two missing disks under the ordinary intact-array model. Qualified two-drive-failure distinction; normally at least four members. Additional corruption, shared control/path failures and rebuild risks remain. No proof of backup, historical rollback or arbitrary disaster tolerance.

### Warm Site

A partly prepared facility has infrastructure but needs further preparation and restored data. Qualified intermediate readiness class; data restoration is not the only possible missing task. More equipment/software/configuration may be needed. No guaranteed ordering of actual recovery times or achieved RTO/RPO from the label.

### Qualitative Risk Analysis

Descriptive categories can summarize risk judgments. HOLD: an unspecified numbered risk matrix is not necessarily qualitative. Ordinal category codes, semi-quantitative scores and measured quantitative values differ; the card omits the distinction needed to classify them.

### Quantitative Risk Analysis

Numeric measures and monetary-loss terms illustrate a quantitative risk approach. Qualified conventional measured/estimated monetary-loss vocabulary, not any numeric label or a definition requiring money in all quantitative analysis. AV/EF/SLE/ARO/ALE need coherent units, loss scope, frequencies and uncertainty; no calibrated model or calculated result is established.

### Risk Acceptance

An acknowledged risk can be retained without adding a further response at that decision point. Qualified informed authorized residual-risk decision, not ignoring risk, removing current controls, abandoning monitoring or proving risk meets constraints. Actual authority and tolerance must be checked; future controls or reassessment remain possible.

### Risk Appetite

An organization has a broad willingness to bear risk while pursuing its goals. Qualified strategic preference, not an individual exception or measured risk outcome. Categories, constraints and governance remain unspecified; a specific threshold or acceptance decision is separate.

### Risk Avoidance

Ending or changing the exposure-producing activity can avoid a specified risk. Qualified response for the identified source of exposure. A change that merely lowers likelihood/impact is mitigation; stopping one activity does not remove every organizational risk or eliminate remaining stored-data/transition obligations. No actual removal is tested.

### Risk Mitigation / Reduction

Controls can lower a risk likelihood or impact. Qualified risk-reduction mechanism, not elimination, guaranteed effectiveness or transfer of responsibility. It can coexist with acceptance/transfer of remaining exposure; controls require evidence and ongoing evaluation.

### Risk Register

A structured record tracks risks, owners, estimates, status and planned responses. Qualified risk-documentation vocabulary. A list is not a control, accepted treatment, complete inventory or accurate measurement merely because it exists. No prescribed universal schema, maintained register or performed assessment is inferred.

### Risk Tolerance

Specific operational limits should be distinguished from broad appetite. HOLD: acceptable variation from risk appetite uses the wrong or ambiguous reference point. Tolerance describes acceptable risk/performance bounds for objectives in context, aligned with appetite; it is not blanket permission to vary outside appetite. The actual objective/threshold basis is missing.

### Risk Transference

Specified financial consequences can be allocated to another party. Qualified financial-transfer example. Actual insurance/contract terms, limits/exclusions, enforceability and residual exposure matter; hiring a provider alone does not prove transfer. Accountability, customer trust and other consequences need not transfer. No real policy or contract determination.

## Distinctions and limited stress checks

Cold/warm/hot site vocabulary describes preparation, rather than achieved restoration or data-loss targets. Equipment, configuration, connectivity, staff, data readiness and dependency activation remain separate operational questions. A hot label proves neither clean synchronized data nor immediate validated failover. Fault tolerance, traffic distribution and placement across regions describe different mechanisms. Shared dependencies can defeat a geographic availability goal.

RAID0 is nonredundant striping, RAID1 mirroring, RAID5 distributed single parity and RAID6 dual parity. Current-copy redundancy is not independently retained backup history, immunity to propagated deletion/corruption, or protection from every controller/site failure. Thirty-two synthetic combinations enumerate whole-drive failure counts for two-drive RAID0/1, three-drive RAID5 and four-drive RAID6 under stipulated healthy-survivor/controller assumptions. Five source-bound reviewer counterexamples record limits; none is an operational or learner test:

- shared_dependency_outage: Two regions share one required dependency which is unavailable. Geographic spread is present but whole service can still be unavailable.
- readiness_not_targets: Site label is known; dependency activation time, data age and restore validation are not. Neither achieved RTO nor RPO follows from the label.
- objective_limit_not_appetite_drift: Broad availability preference; a defined objective downtime limit; a separate authorized decision. Preference, threshold and acceptance decision cannot be substituted.
- numbered_matrix_not_units: Numbers can encode ordinal categories, arbitrary bins or measured likelihood/impact. Digits alone do not identify analysis method or calibration.
- response_mechanisms_not_labels: Provider hired with no defined loss allocation; controls lower likelihood; activity stopped. Transfer requires specified allocation; reducing exposure is not automatically avoidance.

Appetite expresses broad willingness; tolerance specifies acceptable contextual risk or objective-performance bounds aligned with it; acceptance is an authorized decision about a risk. A numbered matrix alone cannot identify qualitative, semi-quantitative or quantitative analysis: ordinal codes and measured quantities differ. The quantitative card is qualified for its specified monetary examples, without claiming all digits are quantitative or all quantitative methods require money. A register documents risks; it does not implement controls. Avoidance removes the specified exposure, mitigation reduces it, and transfer allocates specified financial consequences while residual obligations and consequences remain.
